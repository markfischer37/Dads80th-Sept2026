The plan

How to surprise an 80-year-old.

Keep an international family visit completely secret. Add one aunt, an empty house, luggage, nerves - and very careful timing.
STEP 01
🤫

Keep the secret

No hints. No accidental messages. As far as Mom and Dad knew, we were still in Israel.

STEP 02
✈️

Fly to Canada

We crossed the Atlantic with one goal: arrive without Mom and Dad finding out we were anywhere near Toronto.

STEP 03
🚙

Auntie Rachie to the rescue

Rachie picked us up, got us to the house and let us in while Bubi and Zaidy were still out.

STEP 04
🏠

The almost-perfect hiding place

We slipped out the back when they got home, tried to stay hidden and discovered that surprise plans do not always go exactly as planned.

The moment

Then they got home...

After all the planning, flying and sneaking around, there was only one thing left to do.
